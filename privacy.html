Privacy Policy — Hacker Path

Last updated: 2026-10-22 · Applies to version 0.1.x (Android)

English

Hacker Path does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. The app works entirely offline and has no account system, no server, and no analytics SDK.

What is stored on your device

  • Your progress: level stars, best scores, combo records, day streak.
  • Your settings: interface language.

This information is written to the app's private storage on your device (Android SharedPreferences). It never leaves the device. Uninstalling the app or using Settings → Reset progress deletes it permanently.

What we do not do

  • No account, sign-in, email address, phone number, or name is requested.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or files are accessed.
  • No advertising SDK is included in version 0.1.x.
  • No third-party analytics, crash reporting, or tracking SDK is included.
  • No data is shared with anyone, because none is collected.

Network access

The app does not require network access to function. All quiz content is bundled inside the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. Because no personal data is collected at all, the app does not knowingly collect data from any user, including children.

Changes

If a future version adds advertising or analytics, this policy will be updated before that version is published, and the updated date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact@yuetool.com

隐私政策 — 黑客之路

最后更新：2026-10-22 · 适用于 0.1.x 版本（Android）

本应用不收集、不上传、不出售任何个人数据。 应用完全离线运行，没有账号系统、没有服务器、没有统计 SDK。

保存在你设备上的内容

  • 游戏进度：关卡星级、最佳成绩、连击记录、连续天数。
  • 设置项：界面语言。

这些信息写入应用在你设备上的私有存储（Android SharedPreferences）， 永远不会离开你的设备。卸载应用或在「设置 → 清空进度」中操作，会永久删除它们。

我们不做的事

  • 不要求注册、登录、邮箱、手机号或姓名。
  • 不访问定位、通讯录、相机、麦克风、相册或文件。
  • 0.1.x 版本不包含任何广告 SDK。
  • 不包含任何第三方统计、崩溃上报或追踪 SDK。
  • 不与任何第三方共享数据——因为根本没有收集。

网络访问

应用无需联网即可使用，全部题库内置于应用内。

儿童

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童。由于完全不收集任何个人数据， 本应用不会（也无法）收集包括儿童在内的任何用户数据。

政策变更

若未来版本加入广告或统计功能，会在该版本发布前更新本政策，并修改上方日期。

联系方式
